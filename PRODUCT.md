# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Asset operators use this internal system to process daily asset work. They create and update records, transfer and receive assets, manage leases and distribution, and keep ownership and location data current. The interface should help them complete this work quickly without sacrificing accuracy.

## Product Purpose

This product manages asset operations through one role-aware web application. It succeeds when every asset's state, owner, location, and transaction history remain current, traceable, and auditable.

## Positioning

One reliable record for every asset throughout its operational lifecycle.

## Brand Personality

Clear, calm, and dependable. Interface language is direct and predictable, with English UI copy and Indonesian business formatting where required.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative SaaS styling: gradients, glossy or excessive cards, marketing-style metrics, ornamental motion, and visual effects that distract from operational work.

## Design Principles

1. Make the current asset state and next valid action obvious.
2. Preserve trust through consistent workflows, explicit feedback, and auditable records.
3. Keep frequent operational work fast without hiding important context or validation.
4. Use familiar product controls so operators can focus on the task rather than the interface.
5. Treat light and dark themes as equally supported working environments.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic structure, sufficient contrast, reduced motion, and status cues that do not rely on color alone. Maintain these requirements in both light and dark themes.
